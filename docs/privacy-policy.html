Privacy Policy

Screenshot Pro – Capture & Annotate · Last updated: June 2026

Screenshot Pro is a browser extension for capturing and annotating screenshots. Your privacy is important to us. This extension is designed to work entirely on your device.

Data collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. We do not use analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party telemetry.

How your data is handled

  • Screenshots are processed locally in your browser. They are stored temporarily in IndexedDB on your device only for the current editing session. The extension does not use the Chrome storage permission.
  • Annotations (text, shapes, crops, etc.) exist only in local memory until you copy or download the image.
  • No cloud sync — nothing is uploaded to our servers because we do not operate any servers for this extension.
  • No account — sign-up or login is not required.

Permissions

The extension requests the following permissions solely to provide its features:

  • activeTab — capture the visible area of the tab you are using when you click the extension.
  • tabs — read the active tab’s title and URL to label captures and determine whether full-page capture is supported.
  • scripting — when you choose full-page capture, injects a short script into the active tab you selected to scroll and measure page height. Injection runs only on your request and only on that tab. No page content is sent externally.

Third parties

This extension does not embed third-party scripts that receive your data. Network access is not used to transmit screenshots or personal information.

Children

This extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

We may update this policy if the extension changes. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when that happens.
